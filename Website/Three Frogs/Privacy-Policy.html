    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: October 2026

    1. Who we are

    This policy explains what personal data Three Frogs Boardgame ("Three Frogs", "we", "us") collects through threefrogsboardgame.com (the "Site"), what we do with it, and how we protect it. We're a boardgame café in Surabaya, Indonesia, and we aim to follow Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP).

    2. What we collect

    When you sign up, we store your name, email address, password and chosen avatar, plus the time you verified your email. We never store your password as plain text. We keep a bcrypt hash of it instead.

    For each booking, we store the name you gave, your email, the date and time, and the number of people.

    A session cookie keeps you logged in. We also record IP addresses so we can spot and slow down repeated failed logins and abuse of signup and password reset. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

    3. What we use it for

    We use your data to:

    • run your account, including emailing you a link to verify your address;
    • handle your bookings and cancellations, including a confirmation email when you book and an email if we have to cancel;
    • show our café staff upcoming bookings (booking name, email, date, time and number of people) so they can get your table ready;
    • send you a password reset link when you ask for one;
    • keep the Site secure, for example by spotting repeated login attempts;
    • reply when you contact us.

    We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with marketing partners.

    4. Cookies

    The Site sets one cookie: a session cookie that keeps you logged in as you move between pages. It's marked HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax, so scripts on the page can't read it and your browser sends it only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The Site can't work without it, and there's nothing optional to switch off, so we don't show a cookie banner.

    5. How we protect your data

    • We store passwords as bcrypt hashes, never as plain text.
    • The whole Site runs over HTTPS.
    • Every action that changes your data (logging in, booking, cancelling, changing your avatar) has protection against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).
    • We limit how often anyone can try to log in, sign up, reset a password or verify an email, to slow down automated attacks.
    • When you reset your password, we log your account out on every device, so anyone who was logged in with the old password has to log in again.
    • Our database queries use parameterized statements, which protects against SQL injection.

    No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable, standard steps to keep your data safe from unauthorized access, changes or disclosure.

    6. Who else handles your data

    • Hostinger hosts the Site and its database for us, so your data is stored on their servers.
    • Google Fonts supplies the Site's fonts. Google sees your IP address when your browser fetches them, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

    We don't share your account or booking data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

    7. How long we keep it

    We keep your account details while your account is open, and booking records for our own operations and records. Password reset links expire after one hour, email verification links after 24 hours, and we delete each one once it's used. To have your account and its data deleted, email us (details below).

    8. Your rights

    Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law, you can:

    • ask to see the personal data we hold about you;
    • ask us to correct anything that's wrong (you can change your avatar yourself on your Dashboard);
    • ask us to delete your account and its data;
    • withdraw your consent at any time by closing your account.

    Email threefrogs@threefrogsboardgame.com for any of these.

    9. Children

    Families and players of all ages are welcome at the café. Accounts on the Site are meant for adults, or for children with a parent or guardian involved. We don't knowingly collect account details directly from young children.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version, so check back now and then.

    11. Contact us

    Questions about this policy or your data? Email threefrogs@threefrogsboardgame.com. Our address is on the About page.
