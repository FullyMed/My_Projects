    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: September 2026

    1. Introduction

    This Privacy Policy explains how Three Frogs Boardgame ("Three Frogs", "we", "us", or "our"), a boardgame café in Surabaya, Indonesia, collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use threefrogsboardgame.com (the "Site"). We aim to handle your data in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP).

    2. Information We Collect

    Account information: when you sign up, we collect your name, email address, password, and your chosen avatar. Your password is never stored in plain text — it is hashed with bcrypt before being saved.

    Booking information: when you make a reservation, we collect the name, email, date, time, and number of people for that booking.

    Technical information: we use a session cookie to keep you signed in, and we log IP addresses for security purposes — specifically, to detect and slow down repeated failed login attempts, signup abuse, and password-reset abuse. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    • To create and manage your account;
    • To process and manage your table bookings and cancellations;
    • To send you a password-reset link when you request one;
    • To keep the Site secure — including detecting brute-force login attempts and other abuse;
    • To respond to you if you contact us directly.

    We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with marketing partners.

    4. Cookies

    The Site sets one essential session cookie so you can stay logged in as you navigate between pages. This cookie is marked HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax, meaning it cannot be read by page scripts and is only sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Because this cookie is strictly necessary for the Site to function, we do not show a cookie-consent banner — there is nothing optional to opt out of.

    5. How We Protect Your Information

    We apply a number of technical safeguards to protect your data:

    • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and are never stored or transmitted in plain text;
    • The entire Site is served over HTTPS;
    • Every action that changes your data (booking, cancelling, updating your avatar, logging in) is protected against cross-site request forgery (CSRF);
    • Login, signup, and password-reset requests are rate-limited to slow down automated abuse;
    • All database queries use parameterized statements to protect against SQL injection.

    No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

    6. Sharing Your Information

    We do not sell or rent your personal data. Your information may be processed by:

    • Hostinger, our web hosting and database provider, which stores the Site's data on our behalf;
    • Google Fonts and jQuery's CDN (jsDelivr/code.jquery.com), which the Site loads resources from — these third parties may see your IP address as part of serving that request, under their own privacy policies.

    We do not share booking or account data with any other third party except where required by law.

    7. Data Retention

    We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. Booking records are kept for our operational and record-keeping purposes. Password-reset tokens expire and are deleted automatically after one hour, or immediately after use. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the details below.

    8. Your Rights

    Consistent with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

    • Access the personal data we hold about you;
    • Request correction of inaccurate data (you can update your avatar directly from your Dashboard);
    • Request deletion of your account and associated data;
    • Withdraw consent by closing your account at any time.

    To exercise any of these rights, email us at threefrogs@threefrogsboardgame.com.

    9. Children's Privacy

    Three Frogs welcomes families and players of all ages at our physical café, but creating an account on the Site is intended for adults or with the involvement of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect account information directly from young children through the Site.

    10. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

    11. Contact Us

    If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at threefrogs@threefrogsboardgame.com, or see our About page for our café address.
