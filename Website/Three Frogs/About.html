    About Three Frogs Boardgame

    Who we are

    Three Frogs Boardgame is a cozy café in Surabaya where friends and families come to play. We have over 200 board games on our shelves.

    Why "Three Frogs"?

    Three friends who loved board games and late-night laughs started Three Frogs as a place to put the phones down and play together.

    What we're here for

    We want to get people around a table, playing face to face, and to grow a friendly boardgame community in Surabaya.

    What you can do here

    • Rent a game, or play it here in our play area
    • Book a private table
    • Join our events and game nights

    Opening hours

    • Every day, 12:00 PM to 10:00 PM
